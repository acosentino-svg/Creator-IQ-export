# Boosting Partnership — eligibility, compensation & tiers

*Wayfair Creators · paid boost on Meta & TikTok*

## Eligibility

An **eligible post** meets program content standards (monthly brief + boost criteria), and includes **#WayfairCreator** and **#wayfairelevate** in the caption (plus **#ad** when required).

### Tier activity — eligible posts per month

| Tier | Eligible posts / month | Notes |
|------|------------------------|--------|
| **Entry** (current) | No program minimum | Use **#wayfairelevate** + **#WayfairCreator** on eligible posts; follow the brief |
| **Tier 2** | **7** | Per calendar month |
| **Tier 3** | **12** | Per calendar month |

Eligible ≠ selected for paid boost.

| Tier | First boost in month | Each additional (same month) |

- $100 / $50 boost pay (see table)
