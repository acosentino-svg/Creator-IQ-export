# Boosting Partnership — compensation & tiers

*Wayfair Creators · paid boost on Meta & TikTok*

## How you get paid

When your post is **selected for paid boost**, you receive boost compensation based on your **tier**. Rates are per **calendar month**: one payment for the first selected boost that month, then a lower rate for each additional selected boost in the same month.

| Tier | First boost in month | Each additional boost (same month) |
|------|----------------------|-------------------------------------|
| **Entry** | $100 | $50 |
| **Tier 2** | $150 | $50 |
| **Tier 3** | $200 | $75 |

## Tier benefits

### Entry

- Monthly **boosting brief** (what Paid is pulling on Meta & TikTok)
- Access to **Creator town halls**

### Tier 2

- **$25–$50 bonus** on selected content, when applicable
- **Category product gifting** when the monthly brief calls for specific content (e.g. sideboards for a TikTok push; product gifted in that category, not for every post)

### Tier 3

- **@wayfaircreators** link exposure on selected boosted posts
- **Highlighted creator** in program communications each month
- **Competitions** and special challenges when the program runs them

## Good to know

- Selection follows the monthly brief and creative fit; not every eligible post is boosted.
- Bonuses and gifting apply only when the team designates a post or brief need.
- Tier placement and program rules are set by the WBP team and may be updated.
