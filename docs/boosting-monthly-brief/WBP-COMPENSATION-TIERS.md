# Boosting Partnership — compensation & tiers

*Wayfair Creators · paid boost on Meta & TikTok*

## Eligibility

An **eligible post** meets boosting guidelines, is in the partnership, and includes **#WayfairCreator** and **#WayfairElevate** (+ **#ad** when required).

| Tier | Eligible posts / month |
|------|------------------------|
| **Entry** | No program minimum |
| **Tier 2** | **7** |
| **Tier 3** | **12** |

Eligible ≠ selected for paid boost. WBP tracks tier placement monthly.

## Compensation (gift card)

| Tier | First boost in month | Each additional (same month) |
|------|----------------------|------------------------------|
| **Entry** | $100 | $50 |
| **Tier 2** | $150 | $50 |
| **Tier 3** | $200 | $75 |

## Tier benefits (enticing summary)

### Entry — *in the boost pool*

- **Get paid when selected** — gift cards per boosted post
- **Monthly boosting brief** — what Paid wants on Meta & TikTok
- **Creator town halls** — Q&A with the team

### Tier 2 — *consistent partner (7+ eligible posts/mo)*

- **Higher boost pay** + **$25–$50 bonus** on selected posts when applicable
- **Category product gifting** when the monthly brief needs specific content (e.g. sideboards for TikTok)

### Tier 3 — *top partner (12+ eligible posts/mo)*

- **Top boost rates**
- **@wayfaircreators** exposure on selected boosts
- **Monthly highlight** in program comms
- **Competitions & special challenges** when offered
