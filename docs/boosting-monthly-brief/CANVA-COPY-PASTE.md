## October overview (left)

Align your posts with what Paid is pulling this month. On Meta, we’re continuing fall / Halloween into early October, then shifting to holiday preview. On TikTok, we’re prioritizing pet, sideboards, Halloween, and Wayfair Verified. Evergreen in-room stories work on both platforms all month.

---

## Story structure (left)

1. **Problem → product → benefit → soft CTA** — one clear arc; no filler.  
2. **Match October themes** — use the overview list; native in-room stories (not sale-led spots).

---

## Deliverables checklist (right)

- **15–45 sec** vertical video (Reels / TikTok) — *not* a carousel  
- **Hook in 1–3 sec** — show the Wayfair product; you on camera, hands-on, or POV  
- **Sound-off ready** — simple on-screen text; readable fonts  
- Caption: **#WayfairCreator** + **#wayfairelevate**; **#ad** when required  
- Link in caption or bio if stickers fail  

---

## Core video criteria (footer — one box)

**We boost** creator-native vertical video: informal tone, bright clear footage, styled lived-in space; product visible early and **in use** (not unbox-only) with one clear benefit; brisk pacing and purposeful cuts — hold shots long enough to see the product. Seasonal or GTM moments are fine; we mainly boost **evergreen** stories, not promo-specific creative.

**We usually pass on** carousels, commercial-style ads, unboxing-only or unclear product, weak/late hooks, missing creator presence, dark or chaotic footage, decorative/hard-to-read on-screen text, and videos led by sales, codes, or limited-time offers.
