## October overview (left)

Align your posts with what Paid is pulling this month. On Meta, we’re continuing fall / Halloween into early October, then shifting to holiday preview. On TikTok, we’re prioritizing pet, sideboards, Halloween, and Wayfair Verified. Evergreen in-room stories work on both platforms all month.

---

## Messaging inspiration (left) — creative only; don’t repeat the platform list here

1. **Hook in 1–3 seconds** — problem, benefit, or discovery; show the product immediately.  
2. **Product in use** — styled room, not unbox-only; one clear benefit or use case.  
3. **You on camera** — face-to-camera, hands-on, or POV for trust and demo.  
4. **Evergreen tone** — seasonal mood is fine; sale codes and promo graphics aren’t our main boost pool.

---

## Deliverables checklist (right — use full column; no second overview box)

- Vertical video, **15–45 seconds**, captioned (sound-off friendly)  
- Your captions:  
  - Reels / TikTok: **#WayfairCreator** and **#wayfairelevate**

- Wayfair product clear and in use in a styled space
