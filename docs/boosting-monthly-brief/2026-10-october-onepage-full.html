October overview

Align posts with what Paid is pulling this month:

  • Meta (early Oct): fall & Halloween — product in the first 3 seconds
  • Meta (mid–late Oct): holiday preview — trees, guest-ready, entertaining
  • TikTok: pet, sideboards, Halloween, Wayfair Verified
  • Both: evergreen in-room, in-use stories all month

Story structure & deliverables

  • Arc: problem → product → benefit → soft CTA — one story, no filler
  • 15–45 sec vertical Reels/TikTok — not a carousel
  • Hook 1–3 sec — show Wayfair product; you on camera, hands-on, or POV
  • Sound-off: simple on-screen text; readable fonts; on screen long enough
  • Hashtags: #WayfairCreator + #wayfairelevate · #ad when required
  • Creator link in caption/bio if product stickers fail

Core video criteria

We boost creator-native vertical video — informal tone, bright clear footage, styled lived-in space. Product visible early and in use (not unbox-only) with one clear benefit. Brisk pacing, purposeful cuts; hold shots long enough to see the product. Seasonal/GTM moments are fine; we mainly boost evergreen stories, not promo-specific creative (sales, codes, limited-time offers).

We usually pass on carousels, commercial-style ads, unboxing-only or unclear product, weak/late hooks, missing creator presence, dark or chaotic footage, decorative/hard-to-read on-screen text, and videos led by sales, codes, or LTO offers.

Selection note

We receive more eligible posts than we can boost. October picks follow the priorities above plus creative fit. Posting eligible content does not guarantee boost. Sept survey: 12 of 17 creators asked for clearer monthly guidance — this brief is that guidance.
