October Boosting Brief

October overview

Match Paid priorities this month: Meta — fall/Halloween early Oct, then holiday preview. TikTok — pet, sideboards, Halloween, Wayfair Verified. In-room, in-use video on both all month.

Messaging inspiration

Sample hooks + what to film — rewrite in your voice; product in frame early.

1 · Fall styling
  • “Two swaps and this room finally feels like fall”
  • “Entryway / porch refresh — what actually stayed”

Film: Layer throws + pillows on sofa; runner or chairs at dining; boot tray + rug at door — fluff on camera, not a flat lay.

2 · Halloween decorating
  • “Spooky-neutral porch — cute, not haunted house”
  • “Tablescape my kids aren’t scared of”

Film: Wide porch or dining reveal → hold on Wayfair piece with pumpkins/garland nearby; on-screen: “livable Halloween.”

3 · Setting up for the holidays
  • “Guest room reset before the in-laws weekend”
  • “Entertaining nook I’ll use all season”

Film: Nightstand, bedding, or lamp in spare room; styled sideboard / bar cart for hosting — show Verified badge on screen if the SKU qualifies.

4 · Fall hosting
  • “Fall dinner when you’re one chair short”
  • “Patio hangout before it hits 50°”

Film: Table set for 6+ with extra seating visible; outdoor chairs + throw, or bench that hides hosting clutter — pet in frame if it’s a pet-friendly piece.

We boost
  • Vertical video (Reels/TikTok) — creator-native, not ad-like
  • Product on screen in the first 3 seconds
  • Shown in use in a styled room — not unbox-only
  • Creator on screen, hands-on, or POV
  • Bright footage, steady pacing, readable sound-off text
We pass on
  • Carousels and commercial-style spots
  • Unboxing-only or product not clear
  • Late/weak hooks; no creator presence
  • Dark, chaotic, or hard-to-read footage/text
  • Creative led by sales, codes, or LTO

Deliverables checklist

  • Vertical video, 15–45 seconds, captioned (sound-off friendly) — not a carousel
  • Your captions:
    • Reels/TikTok: #WayfairCreator and #wayfairelevate
    • #ad when required
  • Link in caption or bio if stickers fail
