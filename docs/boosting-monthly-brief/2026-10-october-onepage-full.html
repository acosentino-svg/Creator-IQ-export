October Boosting Brief

October overview

Align your posts with what Paid is pulling this month. On Meta, continue fall/Halloween into early October (product in the first 3 seconds), then shift to holiday preview. On TikTok, prioritize pet, sideboards, Halloween, and Wayfair Verified. Evergreen in-room, in-use stories work on both platforms all month.

Messaging inspiration

1Hook in 1–3 seconds — problem, benefit, or discovery; show the product immediately
2Product in use — styled room, not unbox-only; one clear benefit
3You on camera — face-to-camera, hands-on, or POV
4Evergreen — seasonal mood is fine; sale/code-led posts aren’t in our main boost pool
  • In room + in use
  • Clear, native creator story
  • Bright, easy-to-follow pacing

Deliverables checklist

  • Vertical video, 15–45 seconds, captioned (sound-off friendly) — not a carousel
  • Your captions:
    • Reels/TikTok: #WayfairCreator and #wayfairelevate
    • #ad when required
  • Link in caption or bio if stickers fail

Core video criteria

We boost informal, in-use vertical video with the product visible early. We usually pass on carousels, unbox-only, weak hooks, dark footage, and sale/code/LTO-led creative.
