How to film it

1One arc — problem → product → benefit → soft CTA; no filler
2Hook in 1–3 sec — Wayfair product on screen; you on camera, hands-on, or POV
3Sound-off ready — simple on-screen text; readable fonts; on screen long enough

Deliverables

  • 15–45 sec vertical Reels / TikTok — not a carousel
  • Styled, lived-in space; bright, clear footage; brisk pacing — hold shots long enough to see the product
  • Hashtags: #WayfairCreator + #wayfairelevate · #ad when required
  • Creator link in caption or bio if product stickers fail

Core video criteria

We boost creator-native vertical video — informal tone, product visible early and in use with one clear benefit. Seasonal/GTM moments are fine; we mainly boost evergreen stories, not promo-specific creative (sales, codes, limited-time offers).

We usually pass on carousels, commercial-style ads, unboxing-only or unclear product, weak/late hooks, missing creator presence, dark or chaotic footage, decorative/hard-to-read on-screen text, and videos led by sales, codes, or LTO offers.

Selection note

We receive more eligible posts than we can boost. October picks follow the priorities above plus creative fit — posting eligible content does not guarantee boost. Sept survey: 12 of 17 creators asked for clearer monthly guidance; this brief is that guidance.
