October Boosting Brief

October overview

Align your posts with what Paid is pulling this month. On Meta, lead with fall & Halloween through early October (porch, dining, livable spooky-neutral styling — product in the first 3 seconds), then shift to holiday preview mid–late October (trees, trim, guest rooms, entertaining). All month on Meta: evergreen any category — office, bath, outdoor, storage, seating — in-room, in-use stories.

On TikTok, prioritize pet (pet + product in frame; beds, washable rugs, pet-friendly furniture), sideboards (styled dining, entry, or living — in use, not unbox-only), Halloween (at-home styling, strong opening, product clear early), and Wayfair Verified (show badge on eligible SKUs). Both platforms: evergreen in-room, in-use video all month — not sale-led spots.

Shot ideas: Fall — porch refresh, cozy layers, dining, entryway, livable Halloween. Holiday — tree & trim, spare room, bath upgrades, entertaining, giftable accents. Evergreen — pet nook, sideboard styling, office/storage, outdoor living, Verified SKU.

Messaging inspiration

1Hook in 1–3 seconds — problem, benefit, or discovery; show the Wayfair product immediately
2One clear arc — problem → product → benefit → soft CTA; one story, no filler
3Product in use — styled lived-in room, not unbox-only; one clear benefit or use case
4You on camera — face-to-camera, hands-on, or POV for trust and demo
  • In room + in use · clear, native creator story
  • Bright, easy-to-follow pacing — hold shots long enough to see the product
  • Sound-off: simple on-screen text; readable fonts; on screen long enough
  • Evergreen: seasonal mood is fine; we mainly boost evergreen stories — sales, codes, and LTO-led creative are usually outside the boost pool

Deliverables checklist

  • 15–45 sec vertical video (Reels / TikTok), captioned — not a carousel
  • Styled space; informal creator-native tone; brisk, purposeful cuts
  • Your captions:
    • Reels/TikTok: include #WayfairCreator and #wayfairelevate
    • Add #ad when required (FTC disclosure)
  • Creator link in caption or bio if product stickers fail

Core video criteria

We boost creator-native vertical video — informal tone, bright clear footage, styled lived-in space; product visible early and in use with one clear benefit. Seasonal or GTM moments are fine; we mainly boost evergreen stories, not promo-specific creative.

We usually pass on carousels, commercial-style ads, unboxing-only or unclear product, weak/late hooks, missing creator presence, dark or chaotic footage, decorative/hard-to-read on-screen text, and videos led by sales, codes, or limited-time offers.
