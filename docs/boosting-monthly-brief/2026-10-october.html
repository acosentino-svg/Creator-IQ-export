October Boosting Brief

Program overview

The Boosting Partnership turns your best WCP Elevate videos into paid ads on Meta and TikTok. We select native creator content — vertical, product-forward, and filmed in real spaces — not promo or sale-led spots.

Messaging inspiration

1
Hook in 1–3 seconds — problem, benefit, or discovery; show the product immediately.
2
Product in use — styled room, not unbox-only; one clear benefit or use case.
3
You on camera — face-to-camera, hands-on, or POV for trust and demo.
4
Evergreen over promo — seasonal mood is fine; codes and sale graphics aren’t our main boost pool.

Campaign overview

October goal: align your posts with what Paid is pulling this month. Meta continues fall / Halloween into early October, then holiday preview; TikTok prioritizes pet, sideboards, Halloween, and Wayfair Verified. Evergreen in-room stories work on both platforms all month.

Content we love

Meta · Fall & Halloween (early Oct) Cozy decor, porch, dining, livable Halloween — product in the first 3 seconds.
Meta · Holiday preview (mid–late Oct) Trees, trim, guest-ready rooms, entertaining — evergreen tone, no sale copy.
TikTok · Pet & sideboards Pet-friendly pieces with pet + product visible; sideboards styled and in use.
TikTok · Halloween & Verified At-home Halloween styling; feature Wayfair Verified products and badge when applicable.
Both · Evergreen Office, storage, bed & bath, outdoor living — problem → product → benefit.

Deliverables checklist

  • Vertical video, 15–45 seconds, captioned (sound-off friendly)
  • Your captions:
    • Reels / TikTok: include #WayfairCreator and #wayfairelevate
    • Add #ad (or required disclosure) when applicable for paid partnership
  • Product in use in a styled space; align with your platform’s October priority (above)

Great fits

  • In room + in use
  • Clear, native creator story
  • Bright, easy-to-follow pacing

Usually skipped

  • Unboxing only, busy overlays
  • AI-looking or screen-record content
  • Unclear which Wayfair product is featured
