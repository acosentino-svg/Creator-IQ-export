Wayfair Boosting Brief – October 2026

This brief aligns Boosting Partnership creators with what our paid social teams are actively selecting for Meta and TikTok. We boost native creator video (vertical, 15–45 seconds) that could run as a paid ad. Seasonal mood is welcome; promo-heavy creative (codes, limited-time offers, sale graphics) is not our main boost pool.

Across both platforms: Hook in 1–3 seconds. Show the Wayfair product early, in a real styled space, with you on camera, hands-on, or POV. Add captions — many viewers watch with sound off.

Great fits vs. usually skipped

Great fits

  • Product in a styled space and in use
  • Clear story: problem → product → benefit
  • Bright, informal, native creator tone
  • One clear Wayfair product and use case

Examples: Reel 1, Reel 2

Usually skipped

  • Unboxing only, no lifestyle or use case
  • Hard-to-read or off-brand text overlays
  • AI-looking footage or website screen recordings
  • Unclear which Wayfair product is featured; dark or confusing openings

Eligibility hashtags

#WayfairCreator  ·  #wayfairelevate

Include both tags in your caption (case-insensitive). Use your normal paid partnership disclosure when required (for example #ad); eligibility for boosting follows the two program hashtags above.

How selection works

We receive more eligible posts than we can boost each month. October selections follow the Meta and TikTok priorities on page 1, plus creative fit and a mix of rooms, styles, and price points.

Posting eligible content does not guarantee boost, but it matches what Paid teams are actively pulling right now. Some posts are selected but not every selection runs as a live boost (testing and performance can affect what goes live).

Frequently asked questions

Do Meta and TikTok want the same videos?

Same creative standards, different monthly themes. Use the Meta table for fall, Halloween, holiday preview, and evergreen; use the TikTok table for pet, sideboards, Halloween, and Wayfair Verified.

Can I post sale or Way Day promo content?

You can post sales content for your audience, but paid boost mainly pulls evergreen storytelling — product in room, in use — not codes or limited-time offer graphics.

What if my Reels product tags don’t work?

Use your creator link in caption or bio and mention it on video. We know tagging is frustrating; linking still matters for your commission.

Why wasn’t my video boosted?

Selection depends on monthly priorities, creative fit, and volume. This brief is the shared criteria Paid and WCP align on each month — we cannot review every post individually.

Tips to get boost-ready

  • Film product in use in a styled space — not just unboxed on the floor.
  • Lead with the hook; don’t delay the product reveal.
  • Invest in pieces you’re proud to leave up if not selected (especially when using #ad).
