October Boosting Brief

October overview

Align your posts with what Paid is pulling this month. On Meta, we’re continuing fall / Halloween into early October, then shifting to holiday preview. On TikTok, we’re prioritizing pet, sideboards, Halloween, and Wayfair Verified. Evergreen in-room stories work on both platforms all month.

Messaging inspiration

1
Hook in 1–3 seconds — problem, benefit, or discovery; show the product immediately.
2
Product in use — styled room, not unbox-only; one clear benefit or use case.
3
You on camera — face-to-camera, hands-on, or POV for trust and demo.
4
Evergreen tone — seasonal mood is fine; sale codes and promo graphics aren’t our main boost pool.

Deliverables checklist

  • Vertical video, 15–45 seconds, captioned (sound-off friendly)
  • Your captions:
    • Reels / TikTok: include #WayfairCreator and #wayfairelevate
    • Add #ad when required for paid partnership disclosure
  • Wayfair product clear and in use in a styled space
  • Link in caption or bio (especially if product stickers fail)
