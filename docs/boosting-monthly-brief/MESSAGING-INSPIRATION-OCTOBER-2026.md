# October 2026 — messaging inspiration (fall)

Story **angles** for what to film — not technical specs (those are **We boost** / **We pass on** on the one-sheet).

---

## 1 · Fall styling

Cozy room refresh with layers and textiles. Porch or entryway fall moment. Dining setup for cooler weather.

---

## 2 · Halloween decorating

At-home Halloween that feels fun, not scary. Porch, table, or mantle styling. **Spooky-neutral** mood — lived-in, not a sale spot.

---

## 3 · Setting up for the holidays

Guest-ready spaces (spare room, bath, etc.). Entertaining or hosting nook. Call out **Wayfair Verified** when the product qualifies.

---

## 4 · Fall hosting

Fall dinners and having people over. Extra seating or storage for guests. Outdoor hangout while the weather still allows.

**TikTok-friendly when it fits:** pet + product in frame · sideboard in dining, entry, or living.

---

## Tone

One clear story per video. Your voice — problem or moment → product in use → why it helps. Seasonal is great; don’t lead with codes or limited-time offers.
