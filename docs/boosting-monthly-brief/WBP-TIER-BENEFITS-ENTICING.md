# Boosting Partnership — enticing tier benefits (copy bank)

Use for email, town hall slides, or creator comms. **Facts** match the program doc; tone is benefit-led.

---

## One-liners (why tier up)

| Tier | Headline |
|------|----------|
| **Entry** | Get in the boost pool with clear monthly direction and paid gift cards when you’re selected. |
| **Tier 2** | Earn more per boost, unlock bonuses, and get product for brief-led content. |
| **Tier 3** | Highest pay, Wayfair audience reach, spotlight, and exclusive challenges. |

---

## Entry

**You’re in the program and creating boost-ready content.**

- **Paid boosts** — Gift card when your post is selected ($100 first boost in a month, $50 each additional).
- **Monthly boosting brief** — Know what Meta & TikTok Paid teams want *before* you shoot.
- **Town halls** — Ask the team how selection works and what “great fit” looks like this month.

---

## Tier 2

**You’re posting consistently (7+ eligible posts/month) and driving the partnership forward.**

- **Higher boost pay** — $150 first / $50 additional per month.
- **Bonus upside** — $25–$50 on selected posts when we run bonus-eligible selections.
- **Brief-led gifting** — When we need a category on TikTok or Meta (sideboards, pet, holiday, etc.), we gift in-category so you can deliver the brief without hunting for the right piece.

*Pitch line:* “Tier 2 is for creators who treat boosting like a rhythm — eligible posts every month, better pay when we select you, and product when the brief calls for it.”

---

## Tier 3

**You’re a top partner (12+ eligible posts/month) — priority pay and priority visibility.**

- **Best boost rates** — $200 first / $75 additional per month.
- **Reach beyond your channel** — Selected content can get **@wayfaircreators** exposure.
- **Monthly highlight** — Stand out in program emails/comms to the full roster.
- **Competitions & challenges** — Invited first when we run sales, linking, or creative challenges with extra rewards.

*Pitch line:* “Tier 3 is max earn + max visibility — you’re who we think of for spotlight and challenges.”

---

## Eligibility reminder (short)

Eligible post = boosting guidelines + **#WayfairCreator** + **#WayfairElevate** (+ **#ad** when required).  
Tier 2 = **7** eligible posts/month · Tier 3 = **12** eligible posts/month.  
Eligible ≠ guaranteed boost.
