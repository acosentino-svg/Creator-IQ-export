October Boosting Brief

October overview

Align your posts with what Paid is pulling this month:

  • Meta (early Oct): fall & Halloween — product in the first 3 seconds
  • Meta (mid–late Oct): holiday preview — trees, guest-ready, entertaining
  • TikTok: pet, sideboards, Halloween, Wayfair Verified
  • Both: evergreen in-room, in-use stories all month

Story structure

1Problem → product → benefit → soft CTA — one clear arc; no filler.
2Match October themes — use the overview list; film native, in-room stories (not sale-led spots).

Deliverables checklist

  • 15–45 sec vertical video (Reels / TikTok) — not a carousel
  • Hook in 1–3 sec — show the Wayfair product; you on camera, hands-on, or POV
  • Sound-off ready — simple on-screen text; readable fonts; on screen long enough
  • Caption hashtags: #WayfairCreator + #wayfairelevate; #ad when required
  • Creator link in caption or bio if product stickers fail

Film ideas this month

Fall & hosting Porch refresh, dining setup, cozy layers, livable Halloween
Holiday preview Tree & trim, spare room, entertaining, giftable accents
TikTok angles Pet + product in frame; sideboard styling; Verified badge on eligible items

Core video criteria

We boost creator-native vertical video: informal tone, bright clear footage, styled lived-in space; product visible early and in use (not unbox-only) with one clear benefit; brisk pacing and purposeful cuts — hold shots long enough to see the product. Seasonal or GTM moments are fine; we mainly boost evergreen stories, not promo-specific creative.

We usually pass on carousels, commercial-style ads, unboxing-only or unclear product, weak/late hooks, missing creator presence, dark or chaotic footage, decorative/hard-to-read on-screen text, and videos led by sales, codes, or limited-time offers.
