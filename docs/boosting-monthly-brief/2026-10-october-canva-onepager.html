October Boosting Brief

October overview

Align your posts with what Paid is pulling this month:

  • Meta (early Oct): fall & Halloween — product in the first 3 seconds
  • Meta (mid–late Oct): holiday preview — trees, guest-ready, entertaining
  • TikTok: pet, sideboards, Halloween, Wayfair Verified
  • Both: evergreen in-room, in-use stories all month

Messaging inspiration

1Hook in 1–3 seconds — problem, benefit, or discovery; show the product immediately.
2Product in use — styled room, not unbox-only; one clear benefit or use case.
3You on camera — face-to-camera, hands-on, or POV for trust and demo.
4Evergreen tone — seasonal mood is fine; sale codes and promo graphics aren’t our main boost pool.

Deliverables checklist

  • Vertical video, 15–45 seconds, captioned (sound-off friendly)
  • Your captions:
    • Reels / TikTok: #WayfairCreator and #wayfairelevate
    • #ad when required for paid partnership disclosure
  • Wayfair product clear and in use in a styled space
  • Link in caption or bio if product stickers fail

Film ideas this month

Fall & hosting Porch refresh, dining setup, cozy layers, livable Halloween
Holiday preview Tree & trim, spare room, entertaining, giftable accents
TikTok angles Pet + product in frame; sideboard styling; Verified badge on eligible items

Great fits

  • 15–45 sec vertical video — problem → product → benefit → CTA; no filler
  • Hook 1–3 sec — problem, benefit, or discovery; product shown; no delayed reveal
  • Creator-native — informal tone, styled lived-in space, bright clear footage
  • You in it — face-to-camera, hands-on, or POV
  • Product focus — visible early, in use in a real space (not unbox-only); one clear benefit
  • Pacing — brisk, easy to follow; purposeful cuts; hold shots to see product
  • Sound-off — simple readable on-screen text; on screen long enough
  • Evergreen — seasonal/GTM mood OK; not promo-led (codes, limited-time offers)

Usually skipped

  • Carousels or wrong format/length
  • Looks like a commercial — not creator content
  • Unboxing only; product not in use; unclear which Wayfair item
  • Weak or late hook; product not visible early
  • No creator presence; dark or cluttered footage
  • Chaotic pacing; can’t see the product
  • Hard-to-read, cursive, or decorative on-screen text
  • Promo-specific: sales, codes, limited-time offer creative
