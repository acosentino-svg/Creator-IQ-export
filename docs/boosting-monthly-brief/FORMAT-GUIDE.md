# Which format for the monthly boosting brief?

| Format | Best for | Creators usually… |
|--------|----------|-------------------|
| **1-page Canva** | Quick email, pin in community, “skim in 60 seconds” | Read half of it |
| **2-page PDF** | Email attachment when you want **full detail** without presenting | Save and refer when filming |
| **4–5 slide deck** | **Town hall**, Loom, team walkthrough, Canva “present” mode | Follow along live; revisit slides when posting |

## Recommendation when email is the main channel

**Attach the 2-page PDF** (`October-Boosting-Brief-2-PAGES.pdf`).  
Put the **short summary in the email body** (see `EMAIL-OCTOBER-2026.md`) — don’t try to fit everything on one Canva page.

| Asset | Role in email |
|-------|----------------|
| **Email body** | October priorities + 3 creative bullets + hashtags |
| **2-page PDF** | Tables, ideas to film, examples, FAQ, selection |
| **1-page Canva** | Optional hero image in body only — not required |
| **Deck** | Skip unless you also do a town hall recording |

## Slide jobs (no repetition)

1. **October priorities** — Meta timeline + TikTok themes only  
2. **Boost-ready creative** — specs, messaging pills, great fits vs skipped  
3. **Ideas to film** — room/category examples (not repeating slide 1 word-for-word)  
4. **Deliverables + FAQ** — hashtags, length, selection honesty, links
