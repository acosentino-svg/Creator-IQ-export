## If you want a PDF

**On a computer**

1. Click this link (opens or downloads):  
   https://github.com/acosentino-svg/Creator-IQ-export/raw/cursor/boosting-monthly-brief-cb1a/docs/boosting-monthly-brief/OCTOBER-ONE-PAGER.pdf

**On iPhone**

- PDFs are awkward in Mail. Use **`OCTOBER-ONE-PAGER.png`** instead, or open the link in **Safari** → share → **Save to Files**.
