Boost-ready creative

Specs

  • 15–45 sec vertical video (Reels / TikTok)
  • Hook 1–3 sec — product visible early
  • You on camera, hands-on, or POV
  • Captions on (sound-off friendly)
  • Seasonal mood OK; sale codes / promo graphics not main boost pool

Messaging

1 Problem, benefit, or discovery — show product immediately
2 Styled room, in use — one clear benefit
3 Native creator tone — bright, easy to follow

Great fits

  • In room + in use
  • Clear Wayfair product story
  • Looks like creator content, not a TV ad

Usually skipped

  • Unboxing only · busy overlays
  • AI-looking or screen recordings
  • Unclear which Wayfair product is featured
