Boosting Partnership — eligibility, compensation & tiers

Wayfair Creators · paid boost on Meta & TikTok

Eligibility

An eligible post meets program content standards (see monthly brief + boost criteria), is in the boosting partnership, and includes both #WayfairCreator and #wayfairelevate in the caption (plus #ad or other disclosure when required).

Content buckets (monthly)

Each month’s boosting brief lists what Paid is pulling on Meta and TikTok (e.g. fall styling, Halloween, holiday preview, TikTok themes). Align eligible posts with those content buckets when you can — selection still depends on creative fit and volume.

Compensation (when selected for boost)

When your post is selected for paid boost, you receive payment based on your tier. Rates are per calendar month: first selected boost in that month, then each additional selected boost in the same month.

Tier benefits

Entry

  • $100 first selected boost / $50 each additional (per month)
  • Monthly boosting brief and Creator town halls

Tier 2

  • $150 first selected boost / $50 each additional (per month)
  • $25–$50 bonus on selected content, when applicable
  • Category product gifting for briefed content — e.g. if TikTok needs sideboards, product is gifted within that category

Tier 3

  • $200 first selected boost / $75 each additional (per month)
  • @wayfaircreators link exposure on selected boosted posts
  • Highlighted creator in program communications each month
  • Competitions and special challenges when the program runs them

Good to know

  • We receive more eligible posts than we can boost; monthly brief priorities + creative fit drive selection.
  • Bonuses and gifting apply only when designated — not on every boost.
  • Program rules and tier criteria may be updated by the WBP team.
