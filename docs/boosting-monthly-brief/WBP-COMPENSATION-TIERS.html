Tier benefits

Pay goes up as you tier up — plus perks that help you post smarter and get more visibility.

Entry

You’re in the program and creating boost-ready content.

  • Get paid when you’re selected — gift card compensation for each boosted post ($100 first / $50 additional per month)
  • Monthly boosting brief — see what Paid is looking for on Meta & TikTok before you film
  • Creator town halls — live Q&A with the team; clarity on selection and creative direction

Tier 2

You’re posting consistently and staying in the boost conversation.

  • Higher boost pay — $150 first selection / $50 each additional per month
  • $25–$50 bonus on selected content when the program applies a performance or priority bonus
  • Category product gifting — when a monthly brief needs specific content (e.g. sideboards for TikTok), we gift product in that category so you can film on brief without sourcing it yourself

Tier 3

You’re a top partner — more pay, more reach, more access.

  • Top boost rates — $200 first selection / $75 each additional per month
  • @wayfaircreators exposure — selected boosted posts can be shared to Wayfair’s creator audience, not just your followers
  • Monthly highlight — featured creator in program communications
  • Competitions & special challenges — first access when we run linking, sales, or creative challenges with extra rewards
