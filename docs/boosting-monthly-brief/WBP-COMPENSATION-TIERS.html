Compensation & tiers

Program overview

The Boosting Partnership (WBP) pays creators when eligible content is selected for paid boost on Meta and TikTok. Your tier sets base rates, extras, and program perks. Monthly briefs and town halls share what Paid is pulling that month so you can align posts with boost priorities.

“Initial” = first eligible post selected for paid boost in that calendar month; “additional” = each further eligible selection in the same month. Bonuses and gifting apply only when noted for a given boost or brief.

What each tier includes

Entry

  • Core boost pay: $100 first selection / $50 additional per month
  • Monthly boosting brief (email) with Meta & TikTok priorities
  • Access to Creator town halls

Tier 2

  • Higher base: $150 initial / $50 additional per month
  • $25–$50 bonus on selected content (when applicable)
  • Category gifting for briefed needs — product seeded in the relevant category when the monthly brief asks for specific content (e.g. sideboards, pet, holiday)

Tier 3

  • Top base: $200 initial / $75 additional per month
  • @wayfaircreators exposure for selected boosted posts
  • Monthly highlight in program comms
  • Invited to competitions and special challenges when the program runs them

How it works

  • Selection ≠ payment until boost runs. Pay follows program rules when your post is selected and boosted.
  • Align with the monthly brief to improve fit when your category is in the Paid priority pool.
  • Category gifting (Tier 2+) is tied to active brief needs — not every post; team coordinates when a category push is live.
  • Bonuses (Tier 2+) apply only on designated selected content.
  • Tier 3 visibility — featured posts and monthly highlight are chosen from boosted content that meets creative and brand standards.
