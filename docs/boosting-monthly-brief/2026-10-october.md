## What makes content boost-friendly

WCP Elevate videos can be boosted as paid ads on **Meta and TikTok**. We select posts that look like **creator content**, not commercials.

| | Guideline |
|---|-----------|
| **Length** | **15–45 seconds** — enough for problem → product → benefit → soft CTA |
| **Format** | **Vertical video** (Reels / TikTok). Carousels are not what we boost for paid. |
| **Opening** | **Hook in 1–3 seconds** — state a problem, benefit, or discovery; **show the product** |
| **You** | Face-to-camera, hands-on, or POV |
| **Product** | **Wayfair item obvious and early**, used in a **styled, lived-in** space |
| **Pacing** | Brisk and easy to follow; purposeful cuts; hold shots long enough to see detail |
| **Captions** | Assume **sound off** — especially on Meta; simple, readable text |
| **Tone** | Informal, bright, native — not a polished TV ad |

**Eligibility hashtags:** `#WayfairCreator` and `#wayfairelevate` (both in caption). Use your normal **paid partnership disclosure** when required (e.g. `#ad`); program eligibility follows the two tags above.

## How selection works

We heard you in the **September 2026 boosting survey** (17 responses). Top barriers were: **not knowing if content will be selected**, **unclear what Wayfair wants**, **needing more products to feature**, and **compensation clarity**. **12 of 17** said more guidance would help them post more consistently.

**What to expect**

- We get more eligible posts than we can boost; October picks follow the **priority table** above plus creative fit.
- We aim for **range** — styles, rooms, and price points — not one “look.”
- Following this brief does **not** guarantee boost, but it matches what Paid is **actively pulling** for Meta/TikTok right now.
- **Selection ≠ live boost every time:** some posts are held for testing or deprioritized if creative doesn’t meet ad performance needs; we’re working on clearer status in dashboard/comms over time.

**Practical tips from your peers**

- Invest in **evergreen** pieces you’d be proud to leave up if not selected (several creators noted `#ad` + non-selection hurts reach).
- **Linking:** if Reels product stickers fail, use your **creator link in caption/bio** and call it out on video; we know tagging is frustrating.
- **Gifting:** WCP gifting and smaller pre-season picks help when you don’t have enough product on hand — watch program email for windows and status.
