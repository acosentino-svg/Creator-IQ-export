Core video criteria

We boost creator-native vertical video: informal tone, bright clear footage, styled lived-in space; product visible early and in use (not unbox-only); brisk pacing; simple readable on-screen text. Seasonal/GTM moments OK; mainly evergreen, not promo-specific creative.

We usually pass on carousels, commercial-style ads, unbox-only/unclear product, weak hooks, no creator presence, chaotic or dark footage, decorative text, and sale/code/LTO-led videos.
