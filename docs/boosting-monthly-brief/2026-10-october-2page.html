Great fits vs. usually skipped

Great fits
  • 15–45 sec vertical; problem → product → benefit → CTA
  • Hook 1–3 sec; product shown early
  • Creator-native; informal; styled lived-in space; bright footage
  • Face-to-camera, hands-on, or POV
  • Product in use in real space; one clear benefit
  • Brisk pacing; shots held long enough to see product
  • Simple on-screen text; works sound-off
  • Seasonal/GTM mood OK; mainly evergreen (not promo codes/offers)
Usually skipped
  • Carousels; wrong length; filler
  • Commercial tone; not creator content
  • Unboxing only; product not in use; unclear Wayfair item
  • Delayed hook; dark/cluttered footage
  • No creator presence; chaotic cuts
  • Cursive/decorative/hard-to-read text
  • Sale-, code-, or limited-time-offer-led creative
